Search by what you remember, not what you typed.

Every entry carries one sentence: why you saved it. Cairn searches that sentence as seriously as the command itself, so the words you remember months later are enough.

Typing partial words works, accents don't matter, and if no entry has every word, Cairn shows the closest ones instead of nothing.

Never leave the keyboard.

Open Cairn from anywhere, type, and press Enter to copy. Ctrl Enter pastes straight into the window you came from. Ctrl K shows every action, with its shortcut, so you learn as you go.

Offline by design. Your notes never leave your machine.

Commands hold server names, paths and sometimes passwords. Cairn makes no network requests at all: everything lives in one SQLite file you can copy, back up or delete.
